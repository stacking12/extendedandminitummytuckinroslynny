About Our Extended & Mini Tummy Tuck Services

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about tummy tuck surgery in Roslyn, NY.

What is the difference between a mini and an extended tummy tuck?

A mini tummy tuck generally focuses on the lower abdomen, below the belly button. An extended tummy tuck addresses a larger area, continuing around the hips and flanks to treat more excess skin. Your surgeon will recommend the right option after examining you.

Am I a good candidate for a tummy tuck?

Good candidates are typically in stable overall health, at a steady weight, and bothered by loose skin or a lax abdominal wall that does not respond to diet and exercise. A consultation is the best way to confirm whether the procedure is appropriate for you.

How long is recovery after abdominoplasty surgery?

Recovery varies by procedure and by patient. Many people return to light daily activities within a couple of weeks, while strenuous exercise usually requires more time. You will receive specific aftercare instructions tailored to your procedure.

Will I have a scar after a tummy tuck?

Yes, a tummy tuck leaves a scar, usually placed low on the abdomen where clothing and swimwear can often cover it. Mini procedures generally involve a shorter scar than extended ones. Your surgeon will explain placement and scar care during your visit.
